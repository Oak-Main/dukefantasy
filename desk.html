Week 10 Fantasy Desk

Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay are on bye in Week 10. Set a Thursday replacement for any Commander or Giant you plan to start, and move a Sunday or Monday player into your flex spot to keep a late swap available.

Desk review · 2026-11-06

What Week 8 settled

All 14 games are final. Buffalo beat Baltimore 36–10 and Detroit beat Minnesota 19–15. The Bills and Vikings both enter their Monday, November 9 meeting at 6–1. Those scores count in the standings. Their Week 9 matchup is still ahead as of this review.

  • NFL Week 8 results
  • NFL Week 9 schedule

Jacksonville–Baltimore is a completed Week 9 game

Jacksonville beat Baltimore 33–10 on Thursday, November 5, moving the Jaguars to 6–2 and the Ravens to 4–5. Before that Week 9 game, Baltimore ruled out quarterbacks Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley, and Jacksonville listed running back Bhayshul Tuten as questionable. Those were pregame designations for the completed game, not Week 10 availability reports. Week 10 availability remains unverified pending next week’s official reports.

Three Week 10 lineup deadlines

Washington at the Giants opens Thursday, November 12 at 8:15 p.m. ET. New England and Detroit play in Munich on Sunday, November 15 at 9:30 a.m. ET (6:30 a.m. Pacific), so review those starters Saturday night. The Chargers visit Baltimore on Monday, November 16 at 8:15 p.m. ET. If you wait on a Monday injury decision, reserve an eligible backup from that game.
